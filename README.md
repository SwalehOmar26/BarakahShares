# BarakahShares

BarakahShares is an AI-powered, blockchain-enabled community investment platform designed to help Muslims pool smaller amounts of capital to participate in real-world businesses through a Musharakah-inspired profit-and-loss sharing model. The platform's first use case is a halal poultry farm, where 25 verified participants can contribute KSh 50,000 each through M-Pesa toward a shared KSh 1.25 million funding target. BarakahShares focuses on solving three major challenges faced by small investors: limited individual capital, lack of transparency, and access to Shariah-conscious investment structures.

The platform combines Flutter, Node.js, Supabase, Solidity and Base L2 to provide a transparent investment experience. Investor contributions and important business records can be linked to blockchain transactions, while supporting documents such as feed receipts and invoices can be stored securely with tamper-evident hashes. Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja API supports local payments, Smile ID can provide identity verification, and QR-based batch traceability connects physical poultry production with digital records.

A key feature of BarakahShares is its AI Transparency Assistant. Instead of presenting investors with complicated financial and blockchain data, the assistant can use authorized platform records to answer questions such as “Where was our money spent?”, “How did the farm perform this month?” and “How was my profit share calculated?” AI can also assist with extracting and summarizing information from uploaded receipts and invoices, while financial calculations and transaction authorization remain controlled by deterministic application logic.

BarakahShares introduces a Proof-of-Business approach where important business activities can be supported by verifiable evidence such as payment records, receipts, invoices, blockchain hashes, QR-linked production records and farm monitoring information. This helps investors understand not only how much they invested, but also how the underlying business is operating.

The long-term vision of BarakahShares is to grow beyond poultry farming into infrastructure that can support transparent, community-funded real-world businesses. By combining community capital, responsible AI, digital payments, blockchain auditability and Shariah-conscious financial principles, BarakahShares aims to make participation in productive businesses more accessible and transparent.

# Core Technologies

* Frontend: Flutter
* Backend: Node.js + Supabase
* Database: PostgreSQL
* Blockchain: Solidity + Base L2
* Blockchain Integration: Viem
* Storage: IPFS via Pinata + Supabase Storage
* Payments: Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja API + Kotani Pay
* KYC: Smile ID
* AI: Generative AI for investment transparency and document analysis
* Traceability: QR Codes
* Farm Monitoring: CCTV integration
